Some apps in your workspace can place their own items on your calendar. For
example, with the Cards app installed, every card with a due date appears as an
all-day item on the day it is due.

These items are read-only on the calendar: you can't drag them to a new day or
edit them here, because they aren't calendar events — they belong to the app
that contributed them. Selecting one takes you to the real thing (a card's due
date opens the card), and any change you make there shows up on the calendar
immediately.

## Showing and hiding a feed

Each feed gets its own checkbox in the sidebar, below your calendars. Uncheck
it to clear those items from the grid; check it to bring them back. This only
changes what you see on this device — it doesn't change anything in the app
the items come from.

If you don't see a feed you expect, the app that provides it may not be
installed — the list shows only feeds from apps in your workspace.
