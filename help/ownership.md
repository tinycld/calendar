## Who owns a calendar

The person who creates a calendar becomes its **owner**. An owner can add and
remove members, rename the calendar, and delete it. Other members can be
**viewers** (see events) or **editors** (add and change events), depending on
the role you give them.

A calendar can have more than one owner. To make someone else an owner, open
the calendar's member list and change their role to **Owner**.

## Leaving a calendar you own alone

If you are the only owner of a calendar and nobody else is a member, you can
delete the calendar or make it personal again with no restriction — nobody
else loses access.

## Leaving a calendar other people use

If you are the only owner of a calendar that other people use, TinyCld
protects them from losing access to it:

- **You choose a successor.** When you delete your account and pick someone
  to take over your content, that person becomes the owner of every calendar
  you solely owned. Members keep their access.
- **An admin deletes your data for you.** If an admin removes your account
  and deletes your data instead of reassigning it, the admin becomes the
  owner of those calendars.
- **You delete your account with no successor.** The deletion is refused.
  Make someone else an owner, delete the calendar, or delete your account
  through account settings and choose a successor.

See [Account settings](help://core:account-settings) for the account deletion
flow, including how to choose a successor.
